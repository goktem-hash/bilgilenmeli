Bilgilenmeli

Short, well-researched explainer videos on water scarcity, climate, environment, workplace safety, health, technology and education.

Bilgilenmeli publishes the same explainers in several languages, each on its own YouTube channel:

  • Türkçe — youtube.com/@bilgilenmeli
  • English — youtube.com/@bilgilenmeli-en
  • Deutsch — youtube.com/@bilgilenmeli-de

The channels are managed with VideoMaster, a private publishing tool that uploads videos, captions and thumbnails to these channels through YouTube API Services. It is used only by its operator and is not offered to the public.
